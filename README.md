# How to play
1.Start.
10 cards are given to each player from the deck and a random player starts the round.

2.Play.
The first player to start the round chooses a card, and every player HAS to follow the card's seed for the round. Then the turn is given to the other player.

3.Round finish.
When all players have played, the round finishes and a winner is chosen based on who placed the strongest card.
Then each player grabs 1 card and shows it to the opponent to ensure that the rules of the game get followd

4.Card strenght.
Every card has it's own strenght, weaker cards lose on stronger, so when playing I recommend saving up the strong cards to gather more points

5.Points.
The aim of the game is to have the most points. Points are given at the end of the round to who played the strongest card following the seed

# MORE
The real game is can be played up to 4 players 2vs2,. So you have to collab with your ally but CANNOT talk to each other whilst the game is played.

The only time something is said about the cards is at the start of the round when the first person may say ONE thing about his hand on the seed that he played
